Before you type your first prompt, it helps to know what is actually on the other side of the chat box. In this lesson you will get a simple, honest picture of AI chat assistants: what they are brilliant at, and where they can trip you up.

## So what is ChatGPT, really?

ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Claude are all **AI chat assistants**. You type a message in ordinary language, and they reply in ordinary language. Under the hood, each one runs on a *large language model*: a computer program trained on an enormous amount of text such as books, websites and articles.

From all that reading, the model has learned patterns in how words, sentences and ideas fit together. When you ask a question, it predicts a helpful response one piece at a time, based on those patterns. It is not looking up a single "correct answer" in a database, and it is not a person thinking about your problem. It is a very capable pattern-matcher with a huge vocabulary.

A useful way to think of it: **a fast, well-read assistant who has never met you and sometimes speaks too confidently.**

## What AI assistants are genuinely good at

Here is where they shine for everyday life in India:

| Task | Example |
|---|---|
| Drafting | A leave application to your manager, a polite reminder to a client about a pending payment |
| Summarising | Turning a long circular from your child's school into five bullet points |
| Explaining | "Explain GST input tax credit like I run a small kirana shop" |
| Planning | A weekly meal plan for a family of four on a budget |
| Rewriting | Making a rough WhatsApp message to a customer sound professional |
| Brainstorming | Ten Instagram caption ideas for a Pune home bakery's Diwali hampers |

Notice that none of these require the AI to be perfectly accurate about facts. They are about **words and structure**, which is where these tools are strongest.

## What they are NOT

### Not a search engine

Some assistants can search the web, but their core job is generating text, not finding verified facts. If you ask for the current repo rate or last week's train timings, double-check with an official source.

### Not always right

AI can produce answers that sound confident but are simply wrong. This is called a **hallucination**. It might invent a book title, a court case, or a statistic. We will cover how to catch this in Module 2.

### Not a mind reader

The AI only knows what you tell it in the chat. If you ask "write an email to my boss," it does not know your boss's name, your company, or why you are writing. More context gives better answers. You will learn a simple formula for this soon.

### Not a replacement for your judgement

Think of AI as a first draft, not a final answer. Priya, a teacher in Nagpur, uses it to draft parent notices, then reads every line before sending. That habit is exactly right.

## ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude: which one?

All three are capable, and all three have free versions. The differences change often as the companies update their tools, so do not worry about picking the "best" one.

- **ChatGPT** (by OpenAI) is the most widely known.
- **Gemini** (by Google) works neatly if you already use Gmail and an Android phone.
- **Claude** (by Anthropic) is known for clear, careful writing.

Pick one to start. The skills in this course work across all of them.

## Try it yourself

This takes about 10 minutes and you do not even need an account yet.

1. Think of three tasks from your last week that involved writing, reading or planning. For example: a message to a landlord, reading a long PDF, or planning a trip to Jaipur.
2. Write them down on paper or in your phone's notes app.
3. Next to each one, mark whether it is mainly about **words and structure** (good fit for AI) or **exact, current facts** (needs checking).
4. Keep this list. You will use these real tasks in Lesson 3.

## Key takeaways

- AI chat assistants generate text by predicting patterns; they do not "know" things the way people do.
- They are excellent at drafting, summarising, explaining, planning and rewriting.
- They can sound confident and still be wrong, so always check important facts.
- The AI only knows what you tell it, so context matters.
- ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude all work for this course; just pick one and start.
