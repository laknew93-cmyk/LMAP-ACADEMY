Knowing how to use AI is one thing; actually using it is another. This final lesson gives you a simple 7-day plan so AI becomes a natural part of your routine, plus ideas for where to go next.

## Why a habit matters

Most people try ChatGPT once, get an average answer, and forget about it. The people who benefit are those who use it a little every day until reaching for it feels as natural as opening Google Maps. Small, regular practice beats one long session.

The plan below takes 10-15 minutes a day. Each day focuses on one skill from this course, applied to your real life.

## Your 7-day AI habit plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Write | Use AI to draft one real email or message you need to send today. Edit it in your own voice before sending. |
| 2 | Summarise | Paste in a long article, notice or email thread (anonymised) and ask for 5 bullet points plus action items. |
| 3 | Explain | Pick one topic that has always confused you, such as a work term, a tax concept or a school subject, and ask AI to explain it simply with an example. |
| 4 | Plan | Plan something real: your week, a study timetable, a family function, or a sales push for your business. Ask for a table. |
| 5 | Rewrite | Take something you wrote recently, like your resume summary or a product description, and improve it with the role-task-context-format formula. |
| 6 | Fact-check | Ask a factual question in your field, then use the fact-check routine from Lesson 5 to verify it. |
| 7 | Review | Look back at the week. Which use saved the most time? Save your three best prompts as templates in your notes app. |

### Ideas by who you are

- **Students:** explain tough chapters, create practice questions, plan revision before exams, improve college application essays.
- **Working professionals:** draft emails, summarise meeting notes, prepare for presentations, rewrite reports more clearly.
- **Homemakers and career returners:** plan meals and budgets, prepare for interviews, refresh your resume, learn a new skill step by step.
- **Small business owners:** write WhatsApp replies to customers, create Instagram captions, draft simple policies, plan a festive season offer.

## Tips to make the habit stick

- **Attach it to something you already do.** For example, open your AI assistant with your morning chai, or right after you check email.
- **Keep a prompt notebook.** Every time a prompt works well, save it. In a month you will have a personal toolkit.
- **Use voice.** Most assistant apps let you speak your prompt. This is handy when you are busy or more comfortable speaking than typing.
- **Try your own language.** If you think more clearly in Hindi, Bengali, Kannada or another language, write prompts in it and ask for answers in whichever language you need.
- **Stay safe.** Keep following the privacy and fact-check habits from Lesson 5, every single time.
