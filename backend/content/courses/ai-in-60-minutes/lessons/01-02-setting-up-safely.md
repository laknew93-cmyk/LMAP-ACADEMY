Setting up an AI assistant takes a few minutes, but a few smart choices at the start will protect your privacy for years. In this lesson you will create a free account and check the settings that matter.

## Step 1: Go to the official website or app only

Fake "ChatGPT" apps and websites are common, and some try to steal logins or charge money for a free service. Always use the official sources:

| Assistant | Official website | Official app |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | chatgpt.com | "ChatGPT" by OpenAI |
| Gemini | gemini.google.com | "Google Gemini" by Google |
| Claude | claude.ai | "Claude" by Anthropic |

On the Play Store or App Store, check the **developer name** before installing. If an app asks you to pay to "unlock ChatGPT" before you have even signed up, close it.

## Step 2: Sign up with an email you control

Most assistants let you sign up with a Google account, an Apple ID, or an email address. A few practical tips:

- Use a personal email you check regularly, not a shared family or office one.
- If you sign up with email, you may receive a one-time code. Enter it only on the official site.
- If you use a password, make it unique. Do not reuse your UPI, banking or email password.
- Turn on two-step verification on the email account you used. That email is the key to your AI account.

Gemini is linked to your Google account, so if you already use Gmail, you may be able to start immediately.

## Step 3: Check your privacy and data settings

This is the step most people skip. Every major assistant has settings that control how your conversations are stored and whether they may be used to improve the company's AI models. The exact names and menus change over time, so look for these ideas in **Settings**:

- **Data controls** or **privacy**: an option about whether your chats can be used to train or improve models. Many people choose to switch this off.
- **Chat history** or **activity**: whether your conversations are saved, and for how long.
- **Memory** or **personalisation**: whether the assistant remembers details about you across chats. Useful, but review what it has stored from time to time.
- **Delete chats**: you can usually delete individual conversations or all of them.

Spend two minutes reading what each setting does. You do not need to switch everything off; just make a conscious choice.

## Step 4: Understand free vs paid

Free versions are more than enough for this course and for most everyday use. Paid plans typically offer higher usage limits, newer models, or extra features. Prices and limits change, so check the official site if you are curious, but there is no need to pay to get started.

If you hit a "limit reached" message on a free plan, simply wait a few hours or switch to another assistant for the day.

## Step 5: Learn the screen

Whichever assistant you choose, the screen looks similar:

- **A message box** at the bottom where you type (or tap the mic to speak).
- **New chat** button to start a fresh conversation on a new topic.
- **Chat history** in a side menu, so you can return to old conversations.
- **An attach button** (often a paperclip or plus sign) to upload a file or photo.
- **Copy, regenerate and edit** buttons near each reply.

A good habit: start a **new chat for each new topic**. Mixing your daughter's science project with your shop's GST queries in one long chat confuses the AI.

## Try it yourself

About 10-15 minutes.

1. Choose one assistant: ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude.
2. Install the official app or open the official website, checking the developer name or web address.
3. Sign up and confirm your email.
4. Open **Settings** and find the data/privacy controls, chat history, and memory options. Decide what you want and set them.
5. Start a new chat and type:

```text
Hi! I'm new to using AI. In 5 short bullet points, tell me what kinds of everyday tasks you can help me with.
```

6. Read the reply. Congratulations, that is your first prompt.

## Key takeaways

- Only use official websites and apps; check the developer name before installing.
- Protect the email you sign up with, and never reuse your banking or UPI password.
- Review data, history and memory settings in the first five minutes.
- Free versions are enough to start; paid plans are optional.
- Start a new chat for each new topic.
