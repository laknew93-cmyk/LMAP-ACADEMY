If you run a small business — a boutique, a clinic, a tuition centre, a home food business — a large part of your day goes into answering customers. This lesson shows how to use AI and WhatsApp Business to reply faster, present your products better, and stay personal. Freelancers can use the same ideas as a service for their clients.

## Start with your most common questions

Before touching any tool, list the questions customers ask most. For a Hyderabad home-tiffin service, it might be:

- What's today's menu?
- Do you deliver to Kondapur?
- What's the monthly plan price?
- Can I pause for a week?
- Do you have Jain food options?

Most businesses find that a small number of questions make up a large share of messages. Answering those well, quickly and consistently saves a lot of time.

Use AI to help you organise:

```text
Here are 40 customer messages from the last two weeks (names removed).
Group them into categories, count how many fall in each, and list the
top 10 questions in plain words.

[Paste anonymised messages]
```

Remove customer names and phone numbers before pasting.

## Write great answers once, reuse them always

Draft answers for your top questions with AI, then check every fact yourself.

```text
I run [business] in [city]. Write short, friendly WhatsApp replies for
these questions. Use simple English (and a Hindi version for each).
Max 50 words each. Use only the facts I give - if something is
missing, write [CHECK] instead of guessing.

Facts: [timings, prices, delivery areas, policies]
Questions: [list]
```

The "[CHECK]" instruction is important. It stops the AI from inventing a delivery charge or a refund policy you don't have.

## Setting up WhatsApp Business

The WhatsApp Business app (free to download) has features designed for small businesses. Feature names and menus can change with updates, but these core tools have been available for a long time:

### Business profile

Add your business name, category, description, address, hours, email and website. Customers see this when they tap your name.

### Greeting message

An automatic message sent when someone messages you for the first time or after a period of no contact:

```text
Namaste! Thank you for contacting Annapurna Tiffins. Our menu for the
week and plan prices are in our catalogue. Reply with your area name
and we'll confirm delivery. We reply between 9 am and 8 pm.
```

### Away message

Sent outside business hours, so customers know when to expect a reply.

### Quick replies

Saved answers you insert with a shortcut (typing "/" in the chat in most versions). Save your top ten answers here.

### Labels

Tag chats such as "New enquiry", "Order placed", "Payment pending", "Repeat customer". This acts as a mini-CRM.

### Catalogue

Showcase products or services with photos, prices and descriptions so customers can browse without asking.

## Writing catalogue descriptions with AI

Good descriptions answer questions before they're asked.

```text
Write a WhatsApp catalogue description (max 60 words) for:
Product: Handloom cotton saree, Pochampally ikat, 6.3 m with blouse piece
Price: ₹[price]
Care: hand wash separately first time
Audience: working women 28-50
Tone: warm, honest. No claims like "best quality in India".
```

Then check colour names, dimensions, and care instructions against the actual product.

## Keep it human

Automation should support personal service, not replace it:

- Use quick replies as a starting point, then personalise with the customer's name and context.
- Handle complaints personally. AI can help you draft a calm, apologetic reply, but you decide what to offer.
- Never let an automated system make commitments — refunds, delivery promises, medical advice — that a person hasn't approved.

```text
A customer is upset that their cake arrived 45 minutes late for a
birthday. Draft a sincere apology in under 70 words. Do not offer any
compensation - I'll decide that. Tone: genuine, not corporate.
```

## Beyond WhatsApp: other places AI helps

- **Google Business Profile:** draft replies to reviews (always personalised, never fake), and write posts about offers and updates.
- **Instagram DMs:** create saved replies for common questions.
- **Email:** draft quotations, order confirmations and polite follow-ups.
- **Festive campaigns:** plan Diwali or Eid offers and draft the messages — but only send promotional messages to customers who have chosen to hear from you.

## Respect customer privacy and consent

- Only send broadcast or promotional messages to customers who have agreed to receive them, and make it easy to opt out.
- Don't paste customers' personal details into AI tools unnecessarily.
- Follow WhatsApp's business policies; misuse can get numbers restricted.

## As a freelance service

Everything in this lesson can be packaged as a service: "WhatsApp Business setup: profile, greeting and away messages, 15 quick replies, labels, and a 20-item catalogue — in English and one regional language." Many small businesses use WhatsApp daily but never set these features up.

## Try it yourself

About 20 minutes. Use your own business, or an imagined one in your niche.

1. List the top 10 customer questions (from real messages, or your best guess).
2. Use the "[CHECK]" prompt to draft answers. Fill in or verify every fact.
3. Write a greeting message and an away message.
4. Write catalogue descriptions for three products or service packages.
5. If you have WhatsApp Business, set up the greeting message, away message and five quick replies. If not, save everything in a document as a ready-to-use kit.

## Key takeaways

- Identify your most frequent customer questions first; answering them well saves the most time.
- Use AI to draft answers and descriptions, with an instruction not to guess missing facts.
- WhatsApp Business features — profile, greeting, away messages, quick replies, labels and catalogue — help small businesses respond faster.
- Keep complaints and commitments human, and send promotions only to customers who have opted in.
