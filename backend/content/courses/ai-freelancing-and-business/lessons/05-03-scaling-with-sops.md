At first, every project feels new. By the fifth one, you notice you're repeating the same steps — and occasionally forgetting one. Templates and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) turn your experience into a repeatable system. In this lesson you'll build Project 4: a delivery SOP for one service.

## Why SOPs matter even for a solo freelancer

An SOP is a written, step-by-step process for doing a task the same way every time. It helps you:

- **Deliver consistent quality** — the checklist catches mistakes when you're tired or busy.
- **Work faster** — no reinventing the process each time.
- **Price confidently** — you know how long each step takes.
- **Grow** — if you later hire an assistant or partner with another freelancer, you can hand them a clear process instead of explaining everything from scratch.

For small business owners, SOPs make it easier to train staff and keep service consistent when you're not around.

## What goes into a delivery SOP

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Purpose | What this SOP covers and the outcome |
| Scope | Which service/package it applies to |
| Inputs | What you need from the client before starting |
| Tools | Software and templates used |
| Steps | Numbered steps from kickoff to close, with time estimates |
| AI prompts | The tested prompts used at each step |
| Quality checklist | Your pre-delivery checks |
| Communication templates | Kickoff, confirmation, delivery, revision, close messages |
| Common problems | Known issues and how to handle them |
| Version and date | So you can improve it over time |

You already have many of these pieces from your "Delivery kit": the kickoff questionnaire, quality checklist, message templates, contract and invoice. The SOP pulls them together.

## Example: SOP outline for "Instagram Starter Month"

```text
SOP: Instagram Starter Month (12 posts)        Version 1.0, Oct 2026

PURPOSE: Deliver 12 on-brand Instagram posts with captions, on time,
with consistent quality.

INPUTS NEEDED: Logo, brand colours, 10+ product photos, kickoff form,
signed agreement, 50% advance.

STEPS:
1. Kickoff (30 min): Send questionnaire; hold 20-min call; create brief doc.
2. Confirm plan (10 min): Send plan confirmation message; wait for "confirmed".
3. Content calendar (45 min): Use Prompt A for ideas; pick 12; add local
   dates/festivals; share for approval.
4. Captions (60 min): Use Prompt B with brief and facts; edit every caption.
5. Designs (90 min): Use brand kit template; licensed elements only.
6. Quality check (30 min): Run Checklist Q1 on all posts.
7. Deliver batch 1 (10 min): Send PDF preview + delivery note.
8. Revisions (30 min): One round per batch; out-of-scope -> quote.
9. Repeat 4-8 for batch 2.
10. Close (15 min): Final files, invoice balance, testimonial request.

PROMPTS: [Prompt A, Prompt B - full text]
CHECKLIST Q1: [full checklist]
TEMPLATES: [links]
COMMON PROBLEMS:
- Photos arrive late -> pause timeline, send reminder template, new dates.
- Client wants reels -> offer reels add-on at listed price.
```

Times are estimates you'll refine by tracking real projects.

## Using AI to draft your SOP

Give the AI your rough notes and existing templates, and ask it to structure them:

```text
Turn my notes below into a clear SOP for delivering [service].
Use the sections: Purpose, Scope, Inputs, Tools, Steps (numbered, with
time estimates), AI prompts used, Quality checklist, Communication
templates, Common problems & fixes, Version/date.
Keep my steps and wording where possible; don't add steps I didn't
mention - instead list suggestions separately at the end.

[Paste your notes, checklist, and templates]
```

The instruction to list suggestions separately keeps you in control of your own process.

## Build a template library

Alongside SOPs, organise reusable templates in a clear folder structure:

```text
Business/
  01_Sales/        one-pager, proposal templates, outreach templates
  02_Contracts/    service agreement, small job confirmation
  03_Delivery/     SOPs, kickoff forms, checklists, brand kit templates
  04_Prompts/      tested prompts by service
  05_Finance/      invoice templates, income tracker, reminders
  06_Clients/      one folder per client
```

### A prompt library

Store prompts that consistently give good results, with notes:

```text
Prompt name: Caption drafts - local food business
Use for: Instagram captions, batch of 6
Inputs needed: brand tone, product facts, festival/occasion
Notes: Works better when I paste 2 past captions the client liked.
Last updated: Oct 2026
```

## Ways to grow once your system works

- **Productise:** turn your SOP-backed service into fixed packages with clear prices.
- **Retainers:** convert one-time clients into monthly clients where it makes sense.
- **Raise prices** as your proof and demand grow.
- **Collaborate:** partner with freelancers offering complementary services — a writer and a designer can offer a joint package.
- **Delegate:** once you have SOPs, you can bring in help for specific steps, while you handle client relationships and final quality checks. If you hire, pay fairly and agree terms in writing.
- **Teach and share:** posts about your process build your reputation and attract clients.

Growth brings more responsibility. Keep quality checks with someone you trust, and don't take on more clients than your process can handle.

## Improve your SOP over time

After each project, spend five minutes answering:

1. What went well?
2. What took longer than expected?
3. What did the client ask that I didn't anticipate?
4. What should I add to the SOP or checklist?

Update the version number. Small improvements compound.

## Try it yourself

About 20 minutes to draft; refine later. This is **Project 4**.

1. Choose one service you offer (your core offer from Project 1).
2. Write rough notes of every step you take, from first contact to final invoice.
3. Gather your kickoff questionnaire, quality checklist, message templates and best prompts.
4. Use the SOP prompt to structure everything.
5. Edit the result: add realistic time estimates and at least three common problems with fixes.
6. Save it as "SOP – [Service] – v1.0" in your Delivery folder.

## Key takeaways

- SOPs turn experience into a repeatable, high-quality process — useful even when you work alone.
- A good delivery SOP covers inputs, numbered steps, prompts, checklists, templates and common problems.
- A tidy template and prompt library saves time on every project.
- Grow by productising, retaining, collaborating and delegating — while keeping quality control.
