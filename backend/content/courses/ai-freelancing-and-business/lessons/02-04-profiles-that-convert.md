Clients will look you up before they reply. Your LinkedIn, Upwork, Fiverr or Instagram profile is often their first impression. In this lesson you will build profiles that clearly say who you help and how, using AI to draft and yourself to make them genuine.

## The one rule for every profile

**Write for the client, not about yourself.** "Passionate, hardworking, detail-oriented individual" says nothing a client can use. "I help clinics answer patient WhatsApp queries faster with ready-made replies and FAQs" tells them exactly why to keep reading.

Every profile should answer, within a few seconds:

1. Who do you help?
2. What problem do you solve?
3. What proof do you have?
4. What should I do next?

## LinkedIn

LinkedIn works well for reaching founders, managers, consultants, CA firms, HR teams and B2B businesses, both in India and abroad.

### Headline

Your headline appears next to your name everywhere. Use your niche statement, shortened:

```text
Content writer for CA & tax firms | Blogs, LinkedIn posts & newsletters
```

### About section

Structure it in short paragraphs:

- Line 1–2: who you help and the problem you solve.
- Middle: what you offer, how you work (including a line on AI + human review), and relevant background.
- End: a clear call to action — "Message me here or email ___ for a sample."

### Featured section

Pin your three portfolio pieces with short descriptions.

### Activity

Posting two or three times a week about your niche helps people remember you. Share practical tips, small lessons from projects, and before/after examples (with permission).

## Upwork

Upwork is a marketplace where clients post jobs and freelancers send proposals. It suits writing, data, presentations, automation and transcription work for international clients.

Key profile elements:

- **Title**: specific, like "Data Cleaning & Google Sheets Automation for Small Businesses".
- **Overview**: the first two lines show in search results, so make them count. Speak to the client's problem.
- **Portfolio**: upload your three pieces with case-study descriptions.
- **Specialised profiles**: if the platform offers them, use one per niche rather than mixing everything.
- **Rate**: set based on your pricing lesson; you can adjust per proposal.

Upwork uses a credit system for submitting proposals and charges service fees. These change, so check the current terms before you start.

## Fiverr

Fiverr flips the model: you create fixed-price "gigs" and buyers come to you. It suits clearly packaged services.

A strong gig has:

- **A specific title**: "I will write 10 SEO-friendly product descriptions for your online store".
- **Three packages** matching your Basic/Standard/Premium tiers.
- **Clear requirements**: what the buyer must provide before you start.
- **Honest FAQs**: turnaround, revisions, how you use AI, what is not included.
- **Gig images**: clean, readable thumbnails made in a design tool.

Visibility on new accounts can be slow. Many freelancers use Fiverr alongside direct outreach rather than relying on it alone.

## Instagram

Instagram matters most for visual services — social media management, design, reels scripting — and for reaching local businesses.

- **Bio**: niche statement + location + call to action ("DM 'MENU' for a free sample post").
- **Highlights**: Services, Portfolio, Testimonials, FAQ.
- **Posts**: show your work, explain your thinking, share tips for your niche.

## WhatsApp Business profile

If clients contact you on WhatsApp, set up a WhatsApp Business profile with a business description, email, hours, and a catalogue of your service packages. We cover WhatsApp Business in depth in Module 5.

## Drafting profiles with AI

Feed the assistant your one-pager and portfolio, then ask:

```text
Using the information below, write:
1. A LinkedIn headline under 200 characters.
2. A LinkedIn About section under 250 words, in first person, short paragraphs.
3. An Upwork title and the first two lines of an overview.
4. A Fiverr gig title starting with "I will".
5. An Instagram bio under 150 characters.
Tone: friendly, clear, specific. No buzzwords like "passionate",
"synergy" or "results-driven". Do not invent experience, numbers or clients.

[Paste your one-pager and portfolio summaries]
```

Then edit hard. Add a personal detail only you could write: "I spent four years at the front desk of a Nashik clinic, so I know which questions patients ask most."

## Photo and basic credibility

- Use a clear, friendly photo with good light — a phone camera is enough.
- Keep your name consistent across platforms.
- Add a professional email address, even a simple Gmail with your name.
- Do not buy followers, fake reviews or endorsements. Platforms detect them, and clients notice.

## Try it yourself

About 20 minutes.

1. Choose the two platforms most relevant to your niche (e.g. LinkedIn + Upwork for B2B writing, Instagram + WhatsApp for local social media).
2. Run the AI prompt with your one-pager.
3. Edit the drafts: remove buzzwords, add one specific personal detail, check every claim is true.
4. Update your headline/bio and About/overview on both platforms.
5. Ask a friend to look at your profile for ten seconds and tell you what you do. If they cannot, simplify.

## Key takeaways

- Every profile should quickly answer: who you help, what you solve, your proof, and the next step.
- LinkedIn and Upwork suit B2B and international clients; Fiverr suits packaged services; Instagram suits visual and local work.
- Use AI for first drafts, then add genuine personal detail and remove buzzwords.
- Never fake followers, reviews or experience.
