Sending the same resume to fifty jobs on Naukri and LinkedIn rarely works. Recruiters and screening software look for a match with the specific role. In this lesson you'll use AI to tailor your resume and write a cover letter for a real job post — honestly and effectively.

## The golden rule: AI tailors, it doesn't invent

AI can rephrase, reorganise and highlight your real experience. It must never add skills, degrees, job titles or achievements you don't have. Recruiters check, background verification is common in Indian companies, and interviewers will ask about anything on your resume. An invented line can cost you the job — even after an offer.

Use this instruction in every resume prompt:

```text
Do not add any skill, qualification, tool, employer or achievement that is not in my original resume. If something important for the job is missing, list it separately as a gap instead of adding it.
```

## Step 1: Decode the job description

Before touching your resume, understand what the employer wants. Copy the full job post and ask:

```text
Analyse this job description. Give me:
1. The 5 most important skills or experiences they want, in order of priority
2. Keywords I should naturally include in my resume
3. What the day-to-day work probably looks like
4. Any "hidden" expectations (for example, client handling, travel, shift timings)
[paste job description]
```

This takes two minutes and tells you exactly where to focus.

## Step 2: Match your experience

Now paste your current resume along with the job description:

```text
Compare my resume with this job description. Create a table with: requirement, evidence from my resume (quote it), strength of match (strong / partial / missing). Then suggest which of my experiences I should highlight more.
```

The "missing" rows are useful. Some gaps you can genuinely address — perhaps you used Excel extensively but never mentioned it. Others you should acknowledge and, if relevant, plan to learn.

## Step 3: Rewrite bullet points with impact

Most Indian resumes list duties: "Responsible for handling customer queries." Strong resumes show results. Ask the AI to help:

```text
Rewrite these resume bullet points to show impact. Use strong action verbs and include numbers ONLY where I have provided them. Where a number would help but I haven't given one, add [ADD NUMBER] so I can fill it in honestly.
- Responsible for handling customer queries
- Prepared monthly MIS reports
- Coordinated with vendors for office supplies
```

A possible result: "Resolved [ADD NUMBER] customer queries per day across phone and email, maintaining…" — you then fill in the real figure.

## Step 4: Tailor the summary and skills

The top of your resume matters most. Ask for a tailored professional summary:

```text
Write a 3-line professional summary for my resume, targeted at this Operations Executive role. Base it only on my real experience. Avoid clichés like "hardworking team player" and "result-oriented professional".
```

Then reorder your skills section so the most relevant ones come first, using the employer's own wording where it truthfully applies to you (for example, "MIS reporting" instead of "making reports").

## Special situations

### Career returners

If you've taken a career break — for caregiving, health, relocation or family — you don't need to hide it. Ask AI to help you present it confidently:

```text
I took a 4-year career break (2021-2025) for childcare after 6 years as an accounts executive. During the break I managed household finances, completed an online GST certification, and helped my brother's shop with bookkeeping part-time. Suggest how to present this break on my resume in 2-3 honest lines.
```

Anjali, returning to work in Bengaluru after five years, used this approach. Instead of an unexplained gap, her resume showed a short, confident line about the break and the skills she had kept up.

### Freshers

With little work experience, focus on projects, internships, college roles and certifications. Ask AI to help turn a final-year project or an internship into clear, specific bullet points.

### Government-exam aspirants moving to private jobs

Years of exam preparation build real skills: discipline, structured study, current affairs, reasoning, and often subject knowledge in economics, polity or accounting. Ask AI to help translate these into private-sector language — honestly, without overselling — and to identify roles where they're relevant.

## Step 5: The cover letter

Many Indian applications don't require a cover letter, but a short, specific one helps for roles you really want, for emails to HR, and for startups.

```text
Write a cover letter for this job, using my resume. Rules:
- Under 250 words
- First line: why this company and role specifically (use details from the job post)
- Middle: 2 specific examples from my experience that match their top requirements
- End: a confident, polite closing
- No generic phrases like "I am writing to express my interest"
[paste job post and resume]
```

Then personalise it: add one genuine sentence about why you want to work there, in your own words.

## Formatting and screening software

Many companies use applicant tracking systems that scan resumes. Keep the format simple: clear headings, standard fonts, no tables or graphics for key information, and save as PDF unless asked otherwise. AI is useful for checking keywords — not for designing fancy layouts.

## Try it yourself — Project: tailored resume and cover letter

This is a course project. Allow 20 minutes (more for polishing).

1. Find a real job post on Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed or a company careers page that you'd genuinely apply for.
2. Run the job-description analysis prompt from Step 1.
3. Run the match table from Step 2 with your current resume. Remove your phone number and address before pasting.
4. Rewrite at least five bullet points with the impact prompt, filling in [ADD NUMBER] with real figures.
5. Write a tailored summary and reorder your skills.
6. Generate a cover letter and add one personal sentence.
7. Read the final resume line by line: is every word true? Save both documents and your best prompts as Prompts #11 and #12 in your library.

## Key takeaways

- AI should tailor and sharpen your real experience — never invent anything.
- Start by decoding the job description, then map your experience to it.
- Turn duties into impact statements using real numbers only.
- Career breaks, fresher profiles and exam-prep years can all be presented honestly and confidently.
- Keep formatting simple for screening software and personalise every cover letter.
