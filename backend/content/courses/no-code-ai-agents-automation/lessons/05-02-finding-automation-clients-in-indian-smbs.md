You now have four working projects. This lesson is about turning those skills into paid work: which Indian small and medium businesses need automation most, how to find them, and how to start conversations that don't feel like cold selling.

## Why SMBs are a good market

India has a very large number of small and medium businesses – clinics, CA firms, coaching institutes, real estate brokers, D2C brands, travel agencies, manufacturers, interior designers. Many run on WhatsApp, Google Sheets, Tally, email and a lot of manual copy-paste. They usually can't justify a full-time developer, but a few hours saved every week, or leads answered in minutes instead of days, is easy for them to understand.

Large enterprises have IT teams and long procurement cycles. SMB owners make decisions themselves, often in one meeting. That makes them ideal first clients for an independent automation builder.

## Pick a niche

"I do AI automation for businesses" is hard to remember and hard to trust. "I help real estate brokers in Pune reply to property-portal leads within five minutes" is memorable, specific and easier to sell.

Choose a niche using three questions:

1. **Do they have a repeated, painful process?** (Leads, enquiries, documents, scheduling, reporting.)
2. **Can you reach them?** Family connections, past jobs, your city's business associations, LinkedIn.
3. **Can they pay?** A process tied to revenue (leads, sales, collections) is easier to justify than a nice-to-have.

Some niches with common, automatable pain points:

| Niche | Common pain | Automation from this course |
|---|---|---|
| Real estate brokers | Portal leads go cold | Lead qualification + WhatsApp follow-up |
| Clinics and diagnostic centres | Appointment and FAQ messages | FAQ bot + hand-off |
| CA and CS firms | Document chasing, crowded inbox | Email triage + reminders |
| Coaching institutes | Admission enquiries, parent queries | Lead flow + FAQ bot |
| D2C brands | Support questions, weekly reporting | FAQ bot + report agent |
| Travel agencies | Itinerary enquiries, quote follow-ups | Lead qualification + drafted quotes |
| B2B manufacturers and traders | Enquiry routing, quotation follow-up | Router + CRM + reminders |

You don't have to stay in one niche forever. But one niche makes your first ten clients much easier.

## Build proof before you pitch

Clients buy outcomes they can picture. Create:

- **A demo video (2–3 minutes)** of each project: a lead submits a form, the sheet fills, the WhatsApp/email arrives, the alert pings. Use test data, never real customer data.
- **A one-page case study** for each project: problem, workflow diagram, what it does, estimated time saved using the method from Module 1, and what the business still controls.
- **A free mini-audit offer:** 30 minutes to map one process and show where automation fits.

If you have done work for a real business, even a relative's shop or a friend's startup, ask permission to describe it. Use real results only; never invent testimonials or numbers.

## Where to find clients

### Warm network first

Your first clients will most likely come from people who already know you: former employers, family businesses, friends who run startups, your CA, your doctor's clinic. Send a short personal message:

```text
Hi Sunil uncle, I've been learning to build AI automations for small
businesses – things like instantly replying to enquiries and keeping
leads organised in a sheet. I'm looking for 2-3 businesses to help this
month. Would you be open to a 30-minute chat about how enquiries come
in at your showroom? No obligation – I'd like to see if I can save your
team some time.
```

### Content on LinkedIn and Instagram

Post what you build. A short screen recording of "how this coaching institute's enquiries now get a reply in under a minute" (with permission or using a mock example) attracts owners who have the same problem. Write in plain language about outcomes, not tools; owners care about "no lead missed", not "webhooks".

### Local business groups

Chambers of commerce, trade associations, BNI-style networking chapters, co-working spaces and startup meetups in your city. Offer a 20-minute talk: "5 tasks small businesses can automate this month". Bring a live demo.

### Partnerships

CA firms, web designers, digital marketing agencies and software resellers (Tally, Zoho partners) already serve SMBs and often get asked about automation. Offer to be their automation partner with a referral fee or white-label arrangement.

### Freelance platforms

Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn services and Indian platforms can help, though competition is high. Specific gig titles ("Lead automation for real estate brokers using Make + WhatsApp") do better than generic ones.

## The discovery call

The first call is not a pitch. It's the process-mapping exercise from Module 1:

1. Ask how work flows today and where it hurts. Let them talk.
2. Map one process together, out loud.
3. Identify one quick win you could deliver in one to two weeks.
4. Explain it in their language: "Every enquiry gets a WhatsApp reply in a minute, and your team sees the serious ones first."
5. Agree on a next step: a short proposal within 48 hours.

Avoid overpromising. Don't say "AI will run your business". Say what the workflow does, what it doesn't, and where humans stay in control. Honest scoping wins repeat business.

## Try it yourself

Spend 20 minutes:

1. Choose one niche from the table (or your own) and write a one-line positioning statement: "I help [who] [achieve what] by [automation]."
2. Record a 2-minute screen demo of Project 1 or Project 3 using test data.
3. List 15 businesses in your network or city that fit the niche.
4. Personalise the warm-network message above for three of them, and send it if you're ready.

## Key takeaways

- Indian SMBs run on manual, repetitive processes and make fast decisions – a strong market for automation builders.
- A specific niche and clear outcome-based positioning beat generic "AI automation" offers.
- Show demos and honest case studies; never invent results or testimonials.
- Start with your warm network, then content, local groups, partnerships and platforms.
- Treat the first call as process discovery, and propose one quick win.
