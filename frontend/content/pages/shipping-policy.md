_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

LMAP Academy sells **online courses only**. Nothing is shipped physically, and there are no shipping charges.

## How you receive your course

- **Instant access:** as soon as your payment is confirmed, the course appears under **My learning** in your account. This usually takes a few seconds.
- **Where to learn:** on lmapacademy.org, from any phone, tablet or computer with an internet connection.
- **Free courses** are available immediately after you create a free account and click **Enrol**.
- **Duration of access:** lifetime access for as long as the course is offered, as described in our [Terms and Conditions](/terms/).

## If you don't receive access

If your payment went through but the course doesn't appear within 30 minutes, email [support@lmapacademy.org](mailto:support@lmapacademy.org) with your registered email address and your Razorpay payment ID (it starts with `pay_`). We'll resolve it within one working day.
