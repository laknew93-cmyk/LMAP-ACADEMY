LMAP Academy is an online academy that teaches **practical AI skills** to learners in India. Our courses take you from your very first prompt to automating real work, analysing data with AI, building AI agents without code, and engineering LLM applications in Python.

## What we believe

- **AI is a skill, not a buzzword.** You learn it by doing. Every lesson ends with an exercise and every course ends with projects you can show an employer or client.
- **Context matters.** Our examples come from Indian workplaces and businesses — GST invoices, Naukri and LinkedIn job hunts, WhatsApp Business, MIS reports — not from someone else's market.
- **Anyone can learn this.** Most of our courses need no coding. We especially welcome career returners, students from Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, and professionals who feel left behind by how fast AI is moving.
- **Honesty over hype.** We will never promise you guaranteed jobs or income. We promise practical, up-to-date skills and a clear path to use them.

## How the courses work

Courses are self-paced and you keep lifetime access. Each course is split into short modules with written lessons, worked examples, prompt templates and hands-on exercises. Mark every lesson complete and you receive a certificate with a unique ID that anyone can verify on this website.

Not sure where to start? Take the free [AI in 60 Minutes](/courses/ai-in-60-minutes) course, then pick a path from [all courses](/courses).
